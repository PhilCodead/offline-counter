# 0.20

- Excel names include device-local calendar date, time to milliseconds and the number of exported records.
- Close is disabled and dimmed during both rewind and collection. The service also rejects close actions while running; stopping or completing restores the button.
- Light-mode panels use a translucent pale background when collapsed and a denser milky gradient when expanded. One rounded background is used throughout, with no separate details layer or screen-wide blur. This is a frosted visual approximation, not live background blur.
- Counting and export contents are unchanged.
